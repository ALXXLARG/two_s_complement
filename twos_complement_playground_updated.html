One final aha moment

Why not just add a sign bit?

A sign-magnitude encoding looks intuitive, but ordinary binary addition does not work the same way.

Sign-magnitude — a trap

+3 = 0000 0011
−5 = 1000 0101
raw sum = 1000 1000

Read as sign-magnitude: −8 (wrong). The sign and magnitude need special treatment. It also has both +0 and −0.

Two's complement — a win

+3 = 0000 0011
−5 = 1111 1011
raw sum = 1111 1110

Read as two's complement: −2 (correct). Addition uses the same bitwise adder, and zero has just one representation.
